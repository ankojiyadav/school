Admission Process

Welcome to Aachi Global School's Admission Portal. We are delighted that you are considering our school for your child's education. At Aachi Global School, we aim to provide a nurturing environment where students can grow academically, socially, and emotionally.

Our admission process is designed to ensure that we understand your child's needs and abilities, so we can provide the best educational experience possible. We look forward to welcoming your family to our school community.

Documents Required

  • Completed application form
  • Four recent passport-sized photographs
  • Birth certificate (original and photocopy)
  • Previous academic records/report cards for the last two years
  • Transfer certificate from the previous school
  • Medical record/health certificate
  • Passport copy (for international students)
  • Aadhar card of the student and parents

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the admission process begin for the new academic year?
The admission process for the new academic year typically begins in November of the previous year. However, we accept applications throughout the year based on seat availability.
What is the age criterion for admissions?

The minimum age requirements as of June 1st of the academic year are as follows:

- Pre-KG: 3+ years

- KG: 4+ years

- Grade 1: 5+ years

And so on for higher grades.

Do you provide transportation facilities?
Yes, we provide transportation facilities for students. Our fleet of buses covers major areas in and around the city. Transportation fees are based on the distance from the school.
Is there a waitlist if classes are full?
Yes, if a particular grade has reached its capacity, we maintain a waitlist. Applications are considered in the order they were received as soon as seats become available.
What curriculum does the school follow?
Aachi Global School follows the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) curriculum with additional focus on holistic development through various co-curricular activities.
Do you offer scholarships?
Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships for outstanding academic achievers. Additionally, we have special scholarships for students excelling in sports or cultural activities at the state or national level.
