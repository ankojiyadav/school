How to apply

Six simple steps.

Our process helps us understand your child's needs so we can give them the best experience possible.

Application form

Complete the online form below, or collect one from the school office.

Documents

Submit the required documents along with the application form.

Assessment

Your child is invited for an assessment based on age and grade.

Interview

A personal meeting with the student and parents is scheduled.

Admission offer

If successful, an offer is made within 7 working days.

Fee payment

Confirm admission by paying the fees within the given time.

Documents required

Keep these ready.

Bring originals and photocopies to the school office.

  • Completed application form
  • Four recent passport-size photographs
  • Birth certificate (original and photocopy)
  • Report cards for the last two years
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Medical record / health certificate
  • Passport copy (international students)
  • Aadhaar card of the student and parents
